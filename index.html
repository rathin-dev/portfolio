About

Independent web & app developer

I’m Rathin Baraiya, a developer from Gujarat with a B.Tech in Information Technology from Government Engineering College, Gandhinagar.

My first project on the web was a blog. I built tecnews.in in college, got it approved for AdSense, ranked its gaming news on page 1 of Google, and sold it in 2025.

From January to June 2023 I worked as a full stack developer at InfoLabz, then went freelance. Today I design, build and launch websites and apps for people whose work still runs on paper, phone calls and WhatsApp. I like projects where the software has to be simple enough for everyone in the room.

My productMandli

The digital record book for savings groups.

A mandal is a savings group: members put in money every month, lend to each other at interest, and share the returns. Mandli replaces the handwritten ledger with an app the whole group can see, and works out the interest on its own.

  • Contributions, loans, interest and payouts worked out automatically
  • Members join with a QR code or an invite code
  • Monthly and all-time PDF reports
  • Chairman and Vice Chairman approvals

Free · English & Gujarati · 1K+ downloads
