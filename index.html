01 Own product · Android app, website, admin panel

Mandli

A digital record book for community savings groups.

Role
Design, development, launch
Year
2026
Status
Live on Google Play

A mandal is a savings group. Neighbours or colleagues put in money every month, lend it to each other at interest, and share the returns. For generations the record has been a handwritten ledger, the chopdo. Mandli replaces that book.

  • 1K+downloads
  • 2languages
  • 3parts: app, site, admin
Get it on Google Play Visit the website

Part one

The app

The whole chopdo in every member’s pocket. The chairman sets the rules once, and from then on the app does the arithmetic nobody enjoyed doing by hand.

  • Flutter
  • Dart
  • Riverpod
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Google Sign-In
  • Contributions, loans at interest, penalties, expenses and payouts are worked out automatically, so the book always balances.
  • Members join by scanning a QR code or typing an 8-character invite code.
  • Chairman and Vice Chairman roles, so money never moves on one person’s say.
  • Every member sees the fund and their own history update live.
  • Monthly and all-time PDF reports, in English or Gujarati.
  • A reminder notification before each collection day.
  • A guided way to bring in a mandal that is already mid-cycle, straight from its paper book.
  • Penalties and expenses recorded alongside, so the books are complete.

Part two

The website

The marketing site that sends people to the Play Store. It is exported as plain static files, so it loads fast and is cheap to host.

  • All the copy lives in one content file, ready for English, Gujarati and Hindi.
  • Privacy, support and account-deletion pages that Google Play asks for.
  • Next.js 15
  • React 19
  • Static export
  • Vercel

Part three

The admin panel

A private, read-only dashboard over the live database. I use it to see new mandals on the day they sign up, and to check a group’s numbers when someone asks for help.

How access is locked down
MiddlewareRefreshes the session and sends signed-out visitors to the login page.
Page guardChecks an email allowlist again on every page render.
DataRead with a service key that never leaves the server.
  • New today, this week and this month at a glance.
  • Search by mandal name or invite code.
  • Next.js
  • Supabase Auth
  • Server-side rendering

02 Client work · Custom WordPress theme

Rescue Foundation Gujarat

A helpline-first website for an animal-rescue trust.

Client
Rescue Foundation, Ahmedabad
Role
Design and development
Year
2026

Rescue Foundation answers calls about injured and trapped animals across Gujarat, day and night. So the site has one job before any other: get a worried person through to the helpline.

Visit the live site
  • a

    Helpline first

    Call and WhatsApp are one tap away on every page. They keep working even if the JavaScript or the stylesheet fails to load.

  • b

    Built from scratch

    A custom WordPress theme with no page builder. The trust edits pages, photos and stories from the normal WordPress admin.

  • c

    A speed limit in the build

    The whole site ships under 30 KB of JavaScript, gzipped. If a change goes over that, the build fails.

  • d

    Volunteer sign-up

    A step-by-step application form. Applications and enquiries are saved in the WordPress admin for the team to follow up.

  • e

    Stories, gallery, videos

    Seven programme pages, a photo gallery, and rescue videos that sync from the trust’s YouTube channel on their own.

  • f

    Donations, ready to switch on

    A Razorpay flow written into the theme without a plugin, with payments checked on the server and receipts by email. It goes live once the trust’s payment account is approved.

  • WordPress
  • PHP 8
  • Vite
  • Vanilla JavaScript
  • Razorpay
  • YouTube API
  • Structured data for search

04 E-commerce · Custom WooCommerce theme

Vanisth

An online shop for a raw-honey brand, built to be believed.

Brand
Vanisth raw honey
Role
Design and development
Status
In progress

Adulterated honey is a well-known problem in India, so a raw-honey brand’s hardest job is being believed. I designed the shop like a specimen catalogue, then made the evidence real.

Every jar carries a batch code. Typed in or scanned, it opens that batch’s own page, with the harvest record and the lab report right there.

Theme built. Not on the live shop yet.

Batch pages
Built into the theme, with no plugin. The code on the label is the page address, short enough to print and to turn into a QR code.
The pour
A 36-frame honey pour that plays as you scroll, on phones too. It steps aside on slow connections, data saver, low-memory phones and reduced motion.
Checkout
UPI or bank transfer, confirmed on WhatsApp, and a free-shipping bar that reads the shop’s real shipping settings.
Weight
2 KB of JavaScript, gzipped, and under 50 KB of CSS. The build fails if either goes over its budget.
Editing
Ready-made blocks for each honey, so the pages can change without a developer. No page builder, no jQuery.
Languages
All 183 strings are ready to translate, with Gujarati type already in place.

  • WooCommerce
  • PHP 8
  • Vite
  • Canvas animation
  • Structured data
